Read A Ta Report

Jul 29, 2025

After waking up around 11 AM, I quicly rushed to Loomis to download my 7_8(2) Ta sample’s data for group presentation. Fortunately the results are quite presentable this time. I have measured a Tc ~ 0.5 K and an Ic ~ 13 uA. All consistent with the first test results from Feburary. But that also means it’s time to try something different: Professor told me some new ideas during the group meeting, and I’m ready to try them out this week.

After the meeting, I ate the questionable meal of a mixture of leftover Japanese curry from yesterday + pasta from day before yesterday. Well, turns out not bad. It’s like a sour curry pasta dish. Whatever, it can’t be called delicious but it gets the job done reasonably.

After the meal I just spent the next like, 5 hours watching videos, bruh. When I finally decide to do something it’s dinner time lol. So I just dumped some pasta and fronzen veg into the bowl and boil…drain them…then put on my pasta sauce. That’s it. It felt a bit bland halfway through the eating, so I added in a slice of Amr cheese.

After the meal, I sat on my couch and read the “Romance of Sui and Tang Dynasties” for a few hours, then spent the hour before bed reading this paper:

Link to Ta Report

It’s a report on the general properties of Tantalum and ongoing development of its industrial utilizations as films. It’s a shame my research direction isn’t related to its content, but I still learned quite a lot! In short, the paper claimed that Ta is an element most prized and used for its corrosion-resisent, highly-refractive, high-temp-stable, ductile, and bio-compatible properties.

One of its applications is on joint prosthesis. But Ti really beats out Ta on the market, because Ti’s just cheaper. A lot cheaper.
