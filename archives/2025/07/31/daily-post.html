Visiting Presentations

Jul 31, 2025

At noon I went to my lab as usual. With JR’s help, I successfully modified my VI program on Labview: more specifically, switching the axes on the VI grpah. Awesome!

Then I went to buy a lunchbox from EVO, bought Chong an iced americano upon his request, and headed to Chong’s presentation along with Six and his friend. Chong’s presentation is a basically a one hour and a half poster session. This is a requirement of his scholarship. The location is in a small hall of an alumni center. About a dozen presenters were in it. I quickly located Chong’s stand and listened in, but I’m sorry bro Chemistry is just not for me, holy crap I can’t understand a single shit. After he finishes his lecture to the people around me, I asked him to explain some parts of the project in more generalized terms, and I think I…understood a bit more of what’s happening. Basically bro’s developing a new synthesis method for an organic reaction, which has advantage in its ingredient’s simplicity. However, this method is not economical yet, so that’s something he still needs to work on. (Now that I think of it, this project goal and challenge apply to like, 99% of Chemistry research I think?)

Actually something funny happened before I was at poster session. After working with JR, I thought I might just go to the 2nd floor and check out if our academic advisor is in her office, if so, I might as well ask some questions regarding grad school applications. Well, her door is locked. So I thought that I should just send her an email later…and then she suddenly appeared 10 m in front of me in the hallway. Bruh that’s crazy. Anyways, she recognized me as well despite us only meeting roughly once a year. In the office, she heard my questions and quickly directed me to the grad application advisor. Alright, I didn’t know we have one. That’s convenient. I then talked with that advisor about applications… That actually ends up being a very useful conversation.

At 6 PM, I attended the OCEF revival session. We actually discussed a lot, mostly on our vision of the new org’s identities. previous president left first, and the other person asked me about my availability over next semester. I told her I’ll be busy and probably won’t be a board, but I’m willing to support the effort. She was surprised, she thought I was fully ready to be a co-founder. I’ll admit, I’m a jerk. I should’ve made this claim clear in the first place. And that’s how that finished. To be honest, I kept very pesimistic during the meeting, and part of me just wants this to be over, because I felt like it just won’t work. But then again, I should’ve just not signed up for this at all. It was a big mistake on my part.
