I borrowed Six's bike to get some grilling supplies, and met up with Zephyr at Scott park around 7 PM to do some BBQ. I spent about 20 mins trying to light up my coal with leaves but it just wasn't doing the job. I consulted some black uncles sitting nearby and they immeidately helped me light the fire up. Basically I wasn't using ignition oil: After spreading the coals in the grill, I was supposed to soak the coal with oil first, stick some paper towels in, light the oil-coated coal by igniting the paper, and keep spraying oil + flipping the coals around for the next half an hour to get the coal going. Oh boy...Luckily, our food wans't bad! Zephyr marinated some LA galbi and they taste amazing. I brougth some wings, braed, mushrooms and fishballs. The fishballs tasted the best. Zephyr was originally surprised that the fish balls are grillable, but was soon commending the taste. My wings really needed more flavoring though. I think I may rubbing some soy sauce on them next time as they cook.

Yeah, and I forgot to bring drinks so I later bought half a dozen can of pepsi (220 mL each). Zephyr drank 4 I drank 2. Overall I think this turned out alright. Could be better for sure, as the sky got pretty dark at 7 already and we literally had to use our phones as flashlights, but Zephyr said we can grill at his APT next time, so there's next time!
