I volunteered for the GFX Launch event today, it's basically a welcome outdoor event for the 
new engineering students, hosted by ENG 100 staffs and faculties (ELAs). I signed up from 12-5, 
and guess what, well, I woke up 12:10 PM. So I quickly flung myself out of the apt without brushing teeth 
and got to the location, Bardeen quad. I asked people around where can I find our director, and people told me 
to find him in CIF 39, and I somehow walked to CDL first, the back to CIF and searched the underground first, no, 
then 3rd floor? yes. 3039. Alright, thankfully they're doing an instruction session for the afternoon shift ELAs at the time, 
so I just picked a sit and listened in. Got my shirt, switched it on, then we headed to the (bardeen) quad. 

My job is to host the Game Station 1 stand, but there's some time before that, I snuck out to Cravings and ate lunch there first.
Starting about 2 PM, people start to pour in, and our first job is to guide students into forming a 2 and a 5 shape. Did that by holding 
a sign for 15 mins, had the big photo taken, then I got back to my stand and started hosting. We have 2 activities: Poker number guessing game, and Volleyball. 
Well, I chose to host volleyball, and what follows is about 2 hours of nonstop volleyball sessions, groups after groups of people. But it feels nice. 
The vigor and genuine happiness I see on the new students' face affected me too. I realized that I had been just a bit depressed about myself lately, 
and forgot why I came here in the first place: I came to learn! to grow! and to have fun. 

Anyways, the event ended roughly at 4. Then, I went to check out my future teaching classroom in Davenport Hall. It's a nice room!

Afterwards, came back to apt, did back day and cooked myself noodles with the leftover sauce from yesterday along with soy rolls for protein. Worked a tiny bit 
on my resume and just played RA3 for the rest of the night.
