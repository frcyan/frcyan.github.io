I uninstalled Endfield. 

I think it's very much an unifinished and unpolished product. It tries to combine a lot of mechanics into its gameplay, and the resulting mess is just far from excellent. The story is the biggest let-down though. It's over-dramatic yet anti-climatic even from the very START of a GACHA game. Like bro, this is not execusable. They pulled off such a banger for Arknight yet for Endfield it's just so lame like this? I don't know. And then the base-building is just not that interesting after all. Doesn't feel that rewarding.
