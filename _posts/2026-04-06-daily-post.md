Yesterday I went to Sushi Ichiban and ordered a veggie udon for lunch. After I got my order and sat down to eat, I heard some customers arguing loudly behind me. Eventually I heard the owner shouting at them to get out the store and never come in again, apprantly due to a suspected theft. It sounds like two customers were trying to sneakily steal a couple of bottled water after placing their order. But they are refusing for such actions and demanding to look at the video footage. 

Well, it was starting to get a bit annoying so I went to them and told me to just pay and leave, cuz, it's annoying. Then I just went back to eating. That probably helped a bit, as the owner was quoting me later and succesfuly drove them off after a few mintues. I distinctly remembered the owner said he's been working in here for 16 years, and he knows everything that they're doing. 

Well, after he shooed them off, he came and thanked me, and told me he's seen them do that multiple times, and gave me a tap on my shoulder. Hehe, feels good to be a good samaritan.
