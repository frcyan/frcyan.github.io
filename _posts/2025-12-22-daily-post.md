I did it. I beat Gwyn today. He's a very tough yet easy boss. He has this one move that's very tough: a three or four sword-swing combo starting with a very fast cut. But I figured out later that he always flinches when I hit him twice with my great sword, so I just keep trying to steal quick hits on him and flinch him - triggering space for more hit, etc. And got him! After defeating Gwyn, I was wondering if outside is different, and that promptly ended the game, leading to the Dark Lord ending. Man, I wish I had more time to look around the boss room.
